## T1.9: Dispatch the transactional outbox through Redis Streams

### Objective
Outbox publishes stream entries carrying stable UUID logical job IDs; crash after publish/before acknowledgement redelivers safely; queue records are hints, never proof of completion; all clients close during shutdown. XADD duplicates are permitted but carry the same job UUID; PG completion/lease controls decide execution. XACK follows durable completion/rejection; XAUTOCLAIM redelivery never substitutes for PG lease ownership. Postgres outbox next_attempt_at controls delayed retries.
